About Cozy Crafting

Our Story

Since I was a little girl, I have always found happiness in creating. I love drawing, painting, sewing, making jewelry, working with fabrics, creating with my hands… To me, art has always been a way to explore, learn, and express myself.

Cozy Crafting was born almost by accident. After leaving my job, I started creating magnetic bookmarks that I eventually donated to my local library. My daughter encouraged me to share my creations on Instagram and created the page Cozy Crafts Handmade. But for some reason, I always called it Cozy Crafting. And just like that, without planning it, the name that now represents my creative world was born.

Fifi-Lu also came into my life unexpectedly. A few years ago, I started drawing kawaii frogs, and somewhere between one idea and another, I imagined a little frog who lived inside a strawberry. An old little frog I had drawn came back to life and found her place in this universe.

To me, Cozy Crafting is so much more than creating pretty things. It is a collection of my creative journey, everything I have learned, and the things I am still discovering.

I want to create a space where you can express yourself without fear of being judged, create without limitations, and remember that your creativity does not have to be perfect to be valuable.

A little place to create, breathe, discover yourself, and perhaps find a little more peace, self-love, and confidence within yourself.

Welcome to my cozy little universe. 💜
